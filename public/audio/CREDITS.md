# Sound: provenance

The recorded sounds in this folder come from two packs by **Kenney** (kenney.nl):
- **Impact Sounds:** footsteps on grass, wood and concrete, and soft, wood, mining, metal, plank and punch impacts.
- **RPG Audio:** cloth, creaks, door closes, metal click and latch, chop, leather and belt.

- **Licence:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain), which allows commercial use and redistribution. Credit is not required but is given here.
- **Downloaded:** 2026-11-16. The original archives are in `art-src/audio/raw/` and are not shipped.
- **Renamed only:** each file was renamed to the game's sound name (for example `step_grass0.ogg`). The audio itself is unedited; pitch, level and filtering are applied at play time in `src/audio.ts`.

Wind, rain, water, fire, crickets, drips and creature breath are synthesised at run time from filtered noise.
